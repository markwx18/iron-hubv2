# Iron Hub: the big update (October 2026)

A new look across the whole app, a smarter Live screen, an early warning for stalled lifts, and agents that learn from you. Everything below is live now.

---

## A new look, everywhere

Every screen now has the same warm, dark, "command centre" feel: big numbers, rounded cards, and the detail tucked into folds instead of cluttering the page.

- **Today** is a command centre: the day at the top, your readiness ring with a big Start button, your key lifts at today's weights, what to expect, the agents, your plateau watch and the brief. Raw data folds away at the bottom.
- **Navigation** has a side menu on desktop (it collapses to icons) and a drawer on your phone. The bottom bar is unchanged.
- **Train** (Plan, Log, Library), **Body** (Weight, Fuel, Quality, Ready), **Progress** and **Settings** all got the new layout, described below.
- Screens slide in when you switch tabs, but never when the app refreshes on its own. If your phone is set to reduce motion, the animations are off.

---

## Winter Arc countdown

- **A live countdown to Winter Arc** sits in the top bar: days and hours on your phone, down to the second on desktop and in the last day. It shows from 30 days out.
- **At 5 PM Eastern on Oct 16 it goes live.** The top bar switches to "Winter Arc · Day 1", and for the first three days Today opens on a launch message. Golf's done; the iron gets the hours now.
- **You set the dates.** Edit dates & targets on the Winter Arc card takes a start date and time and an end date and time (Eastern). Leave the end time blank to run to the end of that day. The countdown, the launch and the card all follow.

## Live (your workout screen)

- **Every set now tells you what to aim for**: "Set 1: 160 × 5. Hold 160, aim for 6", with the reason.
- **Reps are pre-filled** with that aim, and the Log button reads "Log 160 × 6", so one tap logs a set. Type something else and yours wins.
- **Plates are worked out for you** from the gear you actually have at the gym (45 lb bars, plates from 2.5 to 45, dumbbells 5 to 120). Between sets it tells you what to change: "pull the 10 and the 2.5, add a 5 each side." A weight your plates can't make is shown with both neighbours instead of being quietly rounded.
- **PR watch** tells you the fewest reps at the called weight that would beat your best.
- **Finish time** estimates when you'll be done, from how fast you really move on that day.
- **Rest** gets a little longer on recovery days or after a low check-in, and previews your next set.
- **Quick buttons**: short on time (trims sets, one tap undoes it), equipment taken (swap sheet), and something feels off (opens pain flags and offers to switch the rest of the session to recover).
- **Screen on** keeps your phone awake mid-workout, if you turn it on.
- **A new layout**: one clean header, a segment per planned set, a big weight and aim, steppers, "Muscles so far", and "Up next". Before you start, you get a short briefing.
- **"Last ate" tap** at the start of a session (under 1h, 1–2h, 2–3h, 3h+). After enough sessions, the app tells you which gap works best for you.
- **Typing is safe**: tapping effort, opening the pain panel or getting a DELTA reply no longer wipes the reps you typed.

## Progress

- **Strength** opens on a hero for one lift at a time: your best, its status, a chart with a forecast, and a plain line like "four weeks out, a session most likely reads 245".
- **Plateau watch** colours every lift green, amber or red, from your real sessions: stalled, slowing, effort creeping up, or missing reps. Tap a lift to see the **likely reasons** (recovery, food, volume, load, consistency), each marked High, Medium or Low and never presented as proven.
- **Notifications** tell you when a lift newly starts to stall, when a PR is in reach today, and when a deload is due.
- **Charts show real events**: deload weeks, calorie target changes, heavy-load weeks, bad recovery stretches, and dips. Calorie changes show as a band across the week they applied.
- **Strength radar** shows how much each muscle group has grown over the last 12 weeks.
- **Recap**: a swipeable story of your year (sessions, sets, pounds moved, strength gained, PRs, bodyweight, your agents and habits). It says "so far" now, and becomes your full year on Dec 31.
- Training time is now tracked per session, with a weekly trend.

## Train

- **Plan** opens on the block you're in ("Day 2 of 5"), a timeline of the blocks around it, the next five days, today's lifts with a Start button, and quick controls to change this block's length or type.
- **Log** shows this week against what you planned, your streak, and an 8-week map of every day. Filter by PRs or by day, tap a session to see its sets, and screenshot import and manual entry are folded into "Log a past session".
- **Library** has search and muscle filters. Each lift shows its status, best and flags. Tap one for its card: why it's flagged, switches for strength mode, form focus and MAXED, and Rename everywhere.

## Body

- **Weight**: your latest weigh-in, big, with your pace and whether you're in the band, a weigh-in box right there, then the chart, where it's heading, and the last few weekly averages.
- **Fuel**: today's calorie and protein rings. **The one-tap calorie and protein buttons moved here** from the weight tab. Your pantry folds away once it's stocked.
- **Family pantry check**: tap Share with family on Fuel and text the link to someone at home. They get a simple tick-list of your foods (no app or account needed), tap what's in the house, and send it back. You see exactly what changed ("In the house now: ground beef · Run out: chicken breast") and tap Apply, or Ignore. It only changes what's in stock; your likes and targets stay yours. On iPhone, if their reply opens in Safari, tap Copy update there, then Paste family update in Fuel.
- **The family link is short now** and opens the tick-list on any phone. The old long link could reach an iPhone with its list cut off, and open the main app instead.
- **Submit goes straight to your app.** Share a new link and their page has a Submit button. Next time you open Iron Hub, the update is in the bell and on Today, ready to review. If Submit ever fails, their page falls back to sending the link.
- **Meal options read like meals.** No more "1/2 rotisserie chicken, 800 cal, 90 g" or two whey shakes: one food is one portion, a plate has one main plus sides, and no plate runs far past a meal. **Snack options** now sit under the meals, built from your snacks.
- **The planner plans your whole day.** Breakfast, a morning snack, lunch, an afternoon snack, dinner and an evening snack, every time; the ones already past fold up as "earlier". Each slot is one plate of normal servings from your pantry, and every food has a drop-down to swap it for something else you have, plus a portion size (½ to 2), ✕ to take it off, and + Add a food. Your changes stay for the day. On a training day it builds around your workout: a meal before, a light top-up an hour out, and a post-workout meal. Type what you've already eaten and the earlier slots count as done.
- **Ready**: your recovery ring against your own usual, HRV, resting heart rate and sleep, a 14-day strip, how recovery affects your PRs, and your recent check-ins.
- The tabs are now Weight, Fuel, Quality and Ready.

- Your gear is listed under Training, summaries mention your strength block and MAXED lifts, and the last time you exported a backup is shown. Export backup and Check for update sit at the bottom.

## The agents

- **Every agent can make changes from chat now, not just ZULU.** Ask and it shows you a card. Nothing changes until you tap Apply.
  - **DELTA** handles training: a lift's next weight, sets, strength mode, Form Focus and MAXED, adding, removing or swapping lifts, today's workout, deloads and experiments.
  - **ECHO** handles food: calorie and protein targets, what's in stock, likes and no's, adding a food to your list, and your workout time for fuel timing.
  - **CHARLIE** handles your schedule: the weekly schedule or cycle order, how long the current plan block runs, renaming a lift, and home days.
  - **ZULU** can do all of it.
- **Every card is checked twice**, when the agent suggests it and again when you tap Apply, with the same rules as the nightly requests. A weight can't jump more than two steps over your last top set, nothing gets added next to a fresh injury flag, and a food you said no to never gets stocked.
- **Undo**: an applied card shows an Undo button for the rest of the day on that device. It won't undo if you've changed the same thing since, so it can't erase your newer edit. Renaming a lift can't be undone from chat, and its card says so.
- **The agents know what today is.** Every agent's chat is told the day, where you are in your plan, whether a deload is on (and that it already gives 2 light sets), exactly what Live will give you lift by lift, and which lifts are locked and why. No more "which day is today?".
- **ZULU sees what the others see**, can look up your history, and hears whether each card was accepted before it replies, so it can't tell you something changed when it didn't.
- **Today-only changes**: ask for fewer sets or a lighter weight "just today" and you get a card that changes today's session only, even on a lift under a reset or an Investigation flag. Your split is untouched and it's gone tomorrow. It works mid-session too, for lifts you haven't started.
- **One card for several lifts**: "D3: Leg Press, Leg Extension, Abductor 3 → 2" is one tap and one Undo. Permanent changes now say so ("Every D3 from now on"), and anything left out is listed on the card with the reason.
- **Refused requests are shown**: a line under the reply says what wasn't changed and why.
- Today's plan line, and the lift list on Train › Plan, show the sets you'll really do: 2 a lift during a deload, and any today-only change. Plan used to show your split's 3 next to deload weights.
- The "Not changed" line under a reply groups lifts that share a reason, and no longer cuts a name off mid-word.
- **Track records**: each agent's past suggestions are scored from your real training afterwards, so you can see how often their advice held up.
- **Plateau diagnosis**: when a lift stalls, DELTA gives its own read on why and what to try first.
- **One question a week**: an agent may ask you something it can't tell from your data. Tap an answer, add a note if you want, or skip. It remembers your answer for 90 days. It never changes anything on its own.
- **Spending cap**: a monthly limit ($20 to start) across both your devices, with a warning at 80%. Automatic runs pause at the cap, and anything you start asks first.
- **Safer suggestions**: the app now rejects changes that clash (for example, adding a deload during a deload, or adding sets next to an injury flag).
- **Smarter progression**: weights no longer jump after one outlier session, and a jump earned on grinders repeats once first. These only ever hold a weight back, never push it up.

## Fixes before Winter Arc (Oct 10)

- **Sync can no longer erase a session.** If one device saved anything before it had caught up with the other, its next sync could overwrite the cloud copy and take the other device's newest session with it, on both devices. It now catches up first, every time. Restoring a backup still replaces the cloud copy.
- **A workout you forget to end is saved.** If you closed the app without tapping End and came back the next day, the whole session was silently dropped. Now it's saved on your next open, dated the day you trained, with a note saying so.
- **Winter Arc**: on day 1 the card no longer says you're behind before your first session, the start day says "today" instead of "0 days out", and Start the next arc after an arc you ended early starts next winter's.
- **New Year**: Progress › Overview keeps your year-in-review recap and the strength radar until your first session of 2027.
- **The 9 PM agent check** no longer skips a night when the one before ran past midnight.
- **Weigh-in typos are caught.** A number nobody weighs (like 1715 for 171.5) is refused, and a jump of more than 10 lb from your last weigh-in asks first.
- An exercise name with an apostrophe no longer breaks its buttons. Screenshot imports match your existing lifts. Photos keep their place if a sync lands mid-upload. "Not now" on the fatigue warning stays dismissed.

## Ending a deload early, and agents that talk to each other (Oct 16)

- **End early now really ends the deload.** Before, only part of the app noticed. Your next session was judged against the deload's RPE 5–6 (which also marked the next same-day call down), Today and Plan still said "deload week", and the agents were told you were still deloading. Now the day you end it is recorded, and from that day everything treats it as normal training: RPE 7–8, your usual rep range, "deload ended" on Today, and an "Ended early" chip on Plan. The deload you already ended is handled too.
- **The agents see what you see.** Every night they get the calendar (today, the next 7 days, where you are in the plan and what comes next), your split with its real sets and rep ranges, the plateau watch colours from Today, and your own notes after recent sessions. In chat, each one also knows what it told you last night.
- **They look back at what happened.** The agents now review your recent deloads, calorie changes and low or high weeks, dips, recovery slumps, PRs and finished experiments. The app works out the before-and-after numbers for each (for example, your main lifts' estimated 1RM in the sessions before a deload against the first two after), and the agents draw conclusions only where those numbers support one. They can also look further back, up to a year of events and six months of WHOOP history.
- **Each agent sounds like itself.** ZULU is a calm head coach, CHARLIE is dry and exact, DELTA is a blunt strength coach, and ECHO is practical about food. The facts and the "how sure to sound" rules haven't changed.
- **A nightly roundtable.** Each agent reads what the others said the night before, and can agree or push back from its own area. ZULU closes the night with a line to each of them. In the Hub's activity log a night now reads as one conversation, under a "Nightly cycle" heading, with replies shown as D → E. It's commentary only: nothing changes unless you approve it.
- **Fresher data before a paid run.** The 9 PM check and the morning brief now catch up with your other device first, even if this one has a change it hasn't sent yet.
- No new API calls. The agents read a little more on each one, roughly 50¢ to $1 a month at most; check the spend card after a week.

## Volume last, and ZULU double-checks (Oct 18)

- **No more extra sets on a lift that's stalling and grinding.** DELTA asked for a 4th set of Cable Row on a lift that had stopped moving, with a third of its sets at grind or fail. The app's own instructions had listed "more sets" as the first fix for a stall. Now they go in order: effort and recovery first, then the rep range or a smaller jump, then a variation, and extra sets last, only when the sets feel easy. An agent can't propose more sets on a stalled lift whose sets are already grinding, from the nightly check or from chat. Taking a set off is always allowed, and today-only changes are unaffected.
- **ZULU double-checks the others.** Each night ZULU reviews the new requests from DELTA, ECHO and CHARLIE. A request it thinks is wrong is held back with a one-line reason, shown in the activity log and under your requests as "Held by ZULU". Tap Show anyway to see it: every check runs again first. No extra API calls; ZULU already reads the night.
- Fixed: a set-count request for one lift used to replace a waiting one for a different lift.

## Winter Arc, week one: deloads stay quiet, strain counts, the Desk, a cleaner Strength chart (Oct 21)

- **No more false "overreached" after a deload.** The fatigue number compared this week with the four weeks before it, and those still held your light deload week, so a normal week read 1.54× and the deload warning came back. The norm now leaves deload weeks out. That warning also quietly made every day an Easy day in Live; that stops too.
- **Deload alerts can be off until a date.** Tap **Quiet for 8 weeks** on the Fatigue card, the Live warning or Settings › Training. Until then nothing suggests a deload: no warning, no "deload looks due", Today's tile says Off. The agents are told it's off and can't propose one. Training load is also left out of today's call while it's off. Your planned deloads still run, and **Turn deload alerts back on** ends it early. It syncs to both devices.
- **Strain before the workout eases it.** If your WHOOP strain is already 14 or more when you start (WHOOP's "High"), the day is at least an Easy day. At 18 or more ("All out") it's at least a Recover day and your last accessory is left out. Live says the strain and when WHOOP read it, and "Train the normal plan instead" undoes it all, the left-out lift included. In the two hours before your workout time the app asks WHOOP for a fresh reading, and Live's briefing has a Refresh button.
- **The agents know what each lift trains.** DELTA and ZULU now get a fixed reference for every lift in your split (a low-to-high fly is the upper chest, a high-to-low fly the lower chest, an incline curl biases the long head, and so on). Where it disagrees with what the model remembers, the reference wins. A fly also no longer counts as triceps volume.
- **The Desk**, a new tab in the Hub next to Agents and Flags. Each night the agents can post an observation, a note to each other or a reply, flag a stretch worth remembering, and add a small chart. The app draws every chart from your own data, so no number on a chart comes from a model. Last night's roundtable sits on top, replies sit under what they answer, and × hides a post. It changes nothing in your plan. No new API calls, a few cents a month more.
- **A cleaner Strength chart.** The forecast now grows out of the trend line instead of dropping from your best. The range is one shaded band, the axis uses round numbers, the dates are short, and the heavy-week and calorie marks sit on their own lanes above the chart with a key under it. The numbers haven't changed.

## Bench Practice is back (Nov 1)

- **A light extra bench for class days.** Tap **Log practice** on the Live screen (before you start a session) or in Train › Log. It offers 2 sets of 8–10 at about 60% of your recent bench e1RM, rounded down (145 until you have recent bench sessions), stopping with 4 or more reps left. You can add a 3rd set; a 4th asks first.
- **It says when to go lighter or skip.** A bench session yesterday or today, bench later today, a deload, an Easy or Recover day, or a beat-up check-in: it drops the weight 10% and says why in one line. A pain flag on a chest, shoulder or triceps lift in the last two weeks takes it off the Live screen.
- **It counts as work, never as a weaker bench.** Practice sets count toward weekly volume, the muscle map and fatigue, so today's call sees them. They never touch your bench trend, your next prescription, the plateau watch or predictions. A practice set reaches your PRs only by beating your record, and the PR log marks it "Bench Practice". DELTA and ZULU see what you did as context. Nothing new costs anything.

## Forecasts (Nov 3)

- **Ask an agent about the future, and the app keeps score.** A new tab in the Hub, between Desk and Flags. DELTA takes lift questions ("when will I bench 275?", as an estimated 1RM or for reps, or "what will my bench be on March 1?"). ECHO takes bodyweight ("what will I weigh on March 1?", "when will I reach 190?"), CHARLIE takes showing up (sessions by a date, how long your streak runs), and ZULU the big picture ("how jacked will I look by summer 2027?").
- **Every call sits beside the app's own trend line.** The form shows you the trend line for free while you fill it in. The agent starts from it, moves off it only for a reason in your data, and says why. Both get scored, so you can see whether the agent beats the plain line.
- **Your plans count.** You can give a planned bench frequency, a planned gain in lb a week, or planned calories. When the forecast is scored, the app checks whether you kept the plan. If you didn't, the result still shows, but it doesn't count against the agent.
- **It's scored when the answer is in.** A date is scored the day you first hit it (how many days early or late). A number is scored on its date (how far above or below). ZULU's big picture comes as 2–4 things the app can measure (bodyweight and your main lifts), and when the date arrives you rate it 1–5. The bell tells you when a forecast settles.
- **The agents learn from their misses.** Each time you ask, the agent sees its record and its biggest misses first, along with what it was thinking when it made them, and says how it's correcting. Its record is in its nightly check and its chat too.
- **From chat and from Goal Roads.** Ask an agent "when will I hit 225?" in chat and it can offer a card that opens the form already filled in. Each Goal Road has an **Ask DELTA for a forecast** button. You can also **Ask again** partway through; the first call still gets scored.
- **Cost:** one call per forecast, about 4¢, and only when you tap Ask. Nothing in your plan changes.

# V5 (November 2026)

## Standards (Nov 12)

The app now decides, from your own numbers, when something is off, and it stays visibly off until it is fixed. No model is involved and it costs nothing.

- **A Standards card leads Today.** Five rows: Bulk pace, Showing up, Lifts, Intake, Recovery. Each is On track, Slipping (amber), Off standard (red), No data, or Held. A row that is amber or red is always open and says why, what it costs you in numbers, and exactly what clears it. The rest sit behind one row of chips.
- **Bulk pace is your rule.** One losing week is amber, a second before it has cleared is red, and two weeks in a row at or above 0.5 lb/week clear it. Only finished weeks count, and a week with fewer than 3 weigh-ins is not judged. Two weeks over 1.6 lb/week is an amber for gaining fast.
- **Showing up.** One scheduled day missed with no reason in 14 days is amber, two is red, and a clean week clears it. Each missed day takes a one-tap reason (sick, travel, planned rest, other), and a day with a reason is not a miss. A session you did a day early or up to two days late on a rest day counts as made up. Neither breaks your streak any more.
- **Lifts** follows the plateau watch for your six main lifts. If you have already approved a fix for a lift that is red, the row stays red but says "Plan in motion", what is being tried and when it is read again, and it stops nagging.
- **Intake** only reads days you logged. An unlogged day is unknown, never "under". Under 4 logged days in 7 it says No data.
- **Recovery** compares WHOOP with your own usual. No WHOOP data is No data, never red.
- **A red cannot be dismissed.** While one is red with nothing being tried, a small "Red" mark sits in the top bar, the bell keeps its item, and the agents are told to be direct about it. You can add a reason to any red; outside Showing up it is a note, and it does not clear anything.
- **ZULU's morning brief leads with a Standard that turned red since the last brief.** Same call as before, no extra cost.
- **You can turn all of it off** in Settings, under V5 switches.

## Time away: trips, sick days and exams (Nov 12)

You can now tell the app about days you will not be training as usual, weeks ahead or after the fact.

- **A "Time away" card on Today.** Tap **Add days**, pick Trip, Sick or Exams, the first and last day, and for a trip whether there is no gym, a hotel gym or a home gym. Your Dec 28 to Jan 1 trip can go in now. Longest: 21 days for a trip, 14 for exams, 10 for sick days. A block can start up to 14 days back, so you can add sick days after they happened.
- **Those days are not misses.** The streak does not break, CHARLIE's sheet lists them as "not counted", and Showing up does not go amber or red for them.
- **The Standards a break skews are held.** A trip holds Showing up and Bulk pace for the trip and 7 days after, because weigh-ins take about a week to settle. Sick days and exams hold Intake as well. A held row says "Held" and until when. Lifts and Recovery are never held.
- **A hold pauses a Standard, it does not clear it.** A red stays red underneath, and a scheduled day you skip in the week after a trip is still a miss; it shows when the hold lifts.
- **On the day**, Today shows "Away, day 2 of 5", and the Train at home button when you said there is a gym.
- **The agents know.** From three weeks before, their calendar lists the block, so nobody calls a planned trip slacking.
- **Nothing on your schedule moves.** It is context only. Remove a block any time and its days count as ordinary days again.
- **Plan my trip (paid, off until you turn it on).** In Settings, under V5 switches, turn on **Plan my trip**. Then a trip gets a button: one call to ZULU, about 3 cents, which writes a note for the days before, each away day, and your first sessions back. It is advice on screen; any change to your plan comes as a normal request you approve. The app does not let it tell you how much strength or muscle a break costs, because it has not measured that.
- **You can turn all of it off** in Settings, under V5 switches (Holds).

## Evidence Library (Nov 12): built, and off until you check it

The app now carries 60 research findings on training and nutrition, each with a link to the paper it came from. It is **off**, and nothing shows it or uses it until you turn it on.

- **Check ten first.** In Settings, under V5 switches, there is a card called Evidence Library. Tap **Show the ten to check first**. For each one, open the source, read the abstract, and see whether the finding says what the paper says. These ten are the ones your trip, your bulk rate, protein, sleep, volume, effort and deloads lean on most.
- **Then turn it on** with the Evidence Library switch above the card (it reads "waiting on you" until you do). It is free.
- **What changes when it is on.** A request or a Desk post from an agent can carry source chips, like "E19 · limited". Tap a chip to open the paper. Tap **What it says** to read the finding. Anything with no source says "Source: coach's judgement".
- **Two different things are kept apart.** The finding is what the paper reports. "What it means for you" is the app's own inference, and it is labelled that way every time. Most findings are marked "limited", because that is the state of the research.
- **The agents only see it if you turn on a second switch**, "Evidence in the agents' prompts". That one is paid: an estimated 15 to 40 cents a month. Until then their prompts are exactly what they were.
- **Plan my trip** uses the findings on short breaks once the library is on. It still may not tell you how much strength or muscle a break costs.

## Analysts and squads (Nov 12)

The Hub's Agents view is new. The circle of four is now four lanes, one for each coach, and each coach has analysts under it.

- **Eight analysts, and they cost nothing.** They are not AI calls. Each one is the app working something out from your data, on your phone, every time you look: ALPHA (strength on your main lifts), FOXTROT (sets per muscle), INDIA (how hard your sets were), BRAVO (bulk pace), JULIET (intake), HOTEL (showing up), GOLF (recovery) and KILO (the evidence library). The names are placeholders; tell me if you want others.
- **Each coach shows the worst thing in its squad**, as a colour, an icon and a short reason: "BRAVO: Losing weight", "HOTEL: 1 day missed". No more "RED" or "AMBER" there, as you asked.
- **Tap a coach** to open its lane and get its report and chat underneath, same as before. A lane opens by itself when something in it is flagged.
- **Tap a tile** to see the numbers behind it, what would change it, and its last 14 days.
- **They agree with the Standards card.** BRAVO, JULIET, HOTEL, GOLF and ALPHA take their level straight from your five Standards, so the Hub and Today can never say different things.
- **FOXTROT only lists.** I tested a "volume is off" warning against your real last 88 days and it would have gone off on more than half of them, because your sets per muscle move a lot from one rotation to the next. So it shows the numbers and warns about nothing.
- **INDIA does not score yet.** That arrives with the exercise selector.
- **Analysts never propose changes and you cannot chat with them.** Ask the coach.

**Two new paid switches, both off** (Settings, V5 switches):
- *Squad readings in the nightly prompts*: each coach gets its analysts' flagged numbers each night. About 3 to 15 cents a month.
- *Lift dossiers from DELTA*: a short note from DELTA on each main lift, about once a week per lift, shown in DELTA's lane. About 10 to 30 cents a month.

If you prefer the old circle, turn off "Analysts and squads" in the same place.

## The Selector and swap trials (Nov 12)

INDIA, the analyst that used to only list how hard your sets were, now scores every lift in your split.

- **Every lift gets a fit score out of 100.** It starts at 100 and loses points for things the app can see in your own sessions: a stall on the plateau watch, a lift whose numbers jump around, a lot of grinders, pain flags, sets you keep leaving out, and two lifts doing the same job on the same day. It is worked out on your phone, free, every time you look. No AI call.
- **Find it in the Hub**, under "Lift fit". Each lift reads Keep, Watch, Try a swap or Swap allowed. Tap a lift to see exactly which rows took points off.
- **Right now: 26 lifts keep, 1 watch (Hanging Leg Raise), none marked.** I replayed the scoring over your last 88 days to set the cut-offs. Only one lift would have been marked in that time: Leg Press for about two weeks in late September, the stretch that ended in a reset.
- **Bench and squat are never swapped and never put on trial.** They can only read Watch.

## The Desk does more (Nov 13)

The Desk (Hub, second tab) used to be only what the four coaches wrote. Now it is where the app's monitoring shows up too.

- **Watches.** Tell the app a number to keep an eye on, and it tells you when it crosses. For example: "bench e1RM below 210 for 2 sessions in a row", or "weekly average bodyweight below 158 for 2 weeks". Tap **Add a watch** on the Desk, pick what to watch, below or above, the number, and how many readings in a row. The app checks it every time you open it, free, with no AI call.
- **A watch only counts from the day you make it.** It never fires on old sessions. Deload sessions and home sessions do not count for a lift. A week only counts once it is over.
- **When one fires** it is pinned at the top of the Desk and shows in the bell. A watch fires once; set it again if you still want it. You can have five open at a time.
- **The analysts' readings are on the Desk as cards.** Any analyst whose reading is flagged, or changed in the last two weeks, gets a card: what it reads, since when, and what would change it. Right now that is BRAVO (bulk pace not cleared yet), ALPHA (squat slowing) and JULIET (under on calories 4 of 7 days). They are worked out by the app each time, never written by an AI.
- **Needs your answer** is pinned on top: an agent's open question (you can answer it right there), a watch a coach proposed, a watch that fired.
- **Filters.** Show one coach's things, or only coaches, or only analysts.
- **Plan of Record.** A card for the week's priorities now sits at the top of the Desk and under the Standards on Today. It is empty until a plan is set.

**One new paid switch, off** (Settings, V5 switches):
- *Watch proposals and wider charts from the coaches*: lets a coach propose a watch on a Desk post (you still tap Accept), chart two more things (fatigue index and total hard sets a week), and hear about it the night one of its watches fires. About 3 to 10 cents a month.

If you want the old Desk back, turn off "Desk: analyst cards and watches" in the same place.

## The plan gets scored (Nov 13)

Until now the agents proposed things and the app scored each proposal alone. Nothing scored the week as a whole. Now it does.

- **A Plan of Record for the week.** At most three priorities. Each one is tied to a Standard and to something the app can actually measure: sessions logged, every scheduled day trained, weigh-ins, bodyweight up on the week, days at your calorie target, days at your protein target, a set at the weight the app called, morning check-ins.
- **You can set one today, free.** On the Desk, the Plan of Record card shows the app's own suggestion, built from your Standards (the flagged ones first). Tap **Set this as the week's plan**. For you this week it suggests: bodyweight up at least 0.5 lb, a squat set at the called weight, and calories at target on 5 days. Tap **Withdraw** to take it back.
- **The app scores it, not an AI.** Each priority reads Met, Partly, Missed, Open (its day has not come yet), Held (you are away) or Not judged (not enough data, which is never counted as a miss). The score is worked out from your logs every time you look.
- **On Today** the plan sits under the Standards. For the first three days of a new week it also shows how last week came out, and the bell does too.
- **A missed priority does not turn a Standard red.** The Standards keep their own rules.
- I replayed the app's suggestions over your last ten weeks. Calories at target on 5 days was never fully met (partly 5 weeks, missed 5). Bodyweight up 0.5 lb was met 7 weeks of 9. The top-set check was met all 4 weeks it came up.

**Two new paid switches, both off** (Settings, V5 switches):
- *Weekly Review: ZULU writes the Plan of Record.* On Sunday night ZULU already writes your letter. With this on, the same call also shows ZULU how its last plans scored and has it write next week's plan. One call, not two. About 2 to 6 cents a month. A plan from ZULU can only use the same measurable checks; anything else is dropped.
- *Monthly Review: standing orders.* Once a month, from the server's evening run, ZULU reads the whole month and writes up to five short lines for each coach on what to emphasise. If you want none of it, turn off "Plan of Record" in the same place.

## One Inbox, and lighter screens (Nov 13)

Things kept arriving on Today (Standards, a plan, questions, trial results, stock checks), so each screen got longer. This stage puts everything waiting on you in one place and caps how much any screen shows at once. No model is involved and it costs nothing.

- **One Inbox.** Everything that waits on you is in one list: requests from the agents, requests ZULU held back, a question, a finished swap trial, an experiment session to answer, the Monday stock check, a pantry update, a watch, the plan suggestion, forecasts, plateau notes, the brief. A red Standard always comes first, then requests (and anything that failed), then the routine things. Each row has a label saying what kind of thing it is, and buttons that act on it right there: Approve and Reject for a request (the same checks as before), Open, and Clear.
- **A request cannot be cleared.** It leaves when you answer it, the same way a red Standard leaves only when it is fixed.
- **The bell is the Inbox.** Its number is the Inbox count, and tapping it opens the same list, most urgent first.
- **Today is five parts, top to bottom:** your orders (the Standards and the Plan of Record), today's session with a Start button, the top three of the Inbox (it skips what already has its own place on Today) with "N more", the agents with the brief, and one fold with everything else (last session, trips and holds, the tiles, today's call, fuel timing).
- **The Hub has five tabs:** **Now** (the Standards in brief, the Plan of Record, the whole Inbox with each request, question and trial as its own card, the week's plan, then Flags), **Agents** (the coaches and their squads, the chat, the brief, the log, usage), **Desk**, **Outlook** (forecasts, experiments, your watches in one line) and **Library** (the weekly letter, the Evidence Library once you turn it on, and the way to the technique cards). Tapping the Hub opens Now.
- **No screen shows more than five cards open (Today six).** The rest sit in one fold at the bottom called "More on this screen", which says how many cards it holds. Open it once and it stays open on this device. Raw data still comes last, and a card that failed to load is never folded away.
- **How much each screen shows is up to you, per device.** Settings, App & data, Detail: **Basic** (the five-card rule), **Intermediate** (eight), **Advanced** (everything open). It is also at the bottom of Today's fold. Basic is the default.
- On your data today: Progress, Fuel and Quality each fold a few cards; Today, the Desk, Forecasts, Plan, Log and the Hub's other views fit as they are.

If you want the old layout back, turn off "Organization: one Inbox, lighter screens" in Settings, under V5 switches.
