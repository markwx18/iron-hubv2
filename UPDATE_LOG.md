# Iron Hub: the big update (October 2026)

A new look across the whole app, a smarter Live screen, an early warning for stalled lifts, and agents that learn from you. Everything below is live now.

---

## A new look, everywhere

Every screen now has the same warm, dark, "command centre" feel: big numbers, rounded cards, and the detail tucked into folds instead of cluttering the page.

- **Today** is a command centre: the day at the top, your readiness ring with a big Start button, your key lifts at today's weights, what to expect, the agents, your plateau watch and the brief. Raw data folds away at the bottom.
- **Navigation** has a side menu on desktop (it collapses to icons) and a drawer on your phone. The bottom bar is unchanged.
- **Train** (Plan, Log, Library), **Body** (Weight, Fuel, Quality, Ready), **Progress** and **Settings** all got the new layout, described below.
- Screens slide in when you switch tabs, but never when the app refreshes on its own. If your phone is set to reduce motion, the animations are off.

---

## Live (your workout screen)

- **Every set now tells you what to aim for**: "Set 1: 160 × 5. Hold 160, aim for 6", with the reason.
- **Reps are pre-filled** with that aim, and the Log button reads "Log 160 × 6", so one tap logs a set. Type something else and yours wins.
- **Plates are worked out for you** from the gear you actually have at the gym (45 lb bars, plates from 2.5 to 45, dumbbells 5 to 120). Between sets it tells you what to change: "pull the 10 and the 2.5, add a 5 each side." A weight your plates can't make is shown with both neighbours instead of being quietly rounded.
- **PR watch** tells you the fewest reps at the called weight that would beat your best.
- **Finish time** estimates when you'll be done, from how fast you really move on that day.
- **Rest** gets a little longer on recovery days or after a low check-in, and previews your next set.
- **Quick buttons**: short on time (trims sets, one tap undoes it), equipment taken (swap sheet), and something feels off (opens pain flags and offers to switch the rest of the session to recover).
- **Screen on** keeps your phone awake mid-workout, if you turn it on.
- **A new layout**: one clean header, a segment per planned set, a big weight and aim, steppers, "Muscles so far", and "Up next". Before you start, you get a short briefing.
- **"Last ate" tap** at the start of a session (under 1h, 1–2h, 2–3h, 3h+). After enough sessions, the app tells you which gap works best for you.
- **Typing is safe**: tapping effort, opening the pain panel or getting a DELTA reply no longer wipes the reps you typed.

## Progress

- **Strength** opens on a hero for one lift at a time: your best, its status, a chart with a forecast, and a plain line like "four weeks out, a session most likely reads 245".
- **Plateau watch** colours every lift green, amber or red, from your real sessions: stalled, slowing, effort creeping up, or missing reps. Tap a lift to see the **likely reasons** (recovery, food, volume, load, consistency), each marked High, Medium or Low and never presented as proven.
- **Notifications** tell you when a lift newly starts to stall, when a PR is in reach today, and when a deload is due.
- **Charts show real events**: deload weeks, calorie target changes, heavy-load weeks, bad recovery stretches, and dips. Calorie changes show as a band across the week they applied.
- **Strength radar** shows how much each muscle group has grown over the last 12 weeks.
- **Recap**: a swipeable story of your year (sessions, sets, pounds moved, strength gained, PRs, bodyweight, your agents and habits). It says "so far" now, and becomes your full year on Dec 31.
- Training time is now tracked per session, with a weekly trend.

---

## Train

- **Plan** opens on the block you're in ("Day 2 of 5"), a timeline of the blocks around it, the next five days, today's lifts with a Start button, and quick controls to change this block's length or type.
- **Log** shows this week against what you planned, your streak, and an 8-week map of every day. Filter by PRs or by day, tap a session to see its sets, and screenshot import and manual entry are folded into "Log a past session".
- **Library** has search and muscle filters. Each lift shows its status, best and flags. Tap one for its card: why it's flagged, switches for strength mode, form focus and MAXED, and Rename everywhere.

---

## Body

- **Weight**: your latest weigh-in, big, with your pace and whether you're in the band, a weigh-in box right there, then the chart, where it's heading, and the last few weekly averages.
- **Fuel**: today's calorie and protein rings. **The one-tap calorie and protein buttons moved here** from the weight tab. Your pantry folds away once it's stocked.
- **Family pantry check**: tap Share with family on Fuel and text the link to someone at home. They get a simple tick-list of your foods (no app or account needed), tap what's in the house, and send it back. You see exactly what changed ("In the house now: ground beef · Run out: chicken breast") and tap Apply, or Ignore. It only changes what's in stock; your likes and targets stay yours. On iPhone, if their reply opens in Safari, tap Copy update there, then Paste family update in Fuel.
- **Ready**: your recovery ring against your own usual, HRV, resting heart rate and sleep, a 14-day strip, how recovery affects your PRs, and your recent check-ins.
- The tabs are now Weight, Fuel, Quality and Ready.

## Settings

- **Connections at a glance** comes first: sync, the WHOOP relay, your API key with this month's spend, and the schedule doc. A problem shows red without opening anything.
- Your gear is listed under Training, summaries mention your strength block and MAXED lifts, and the last time you exported a backup is shown. Export backup and Check for update sit at the bottom.

---

## The agents

- **Track records**: each agent's past suggestions are scored from your real training afterwards, so you can see how often their advice held up.
- **Plateau diagnosis**: when a lift stalls, DELTA gives its own read on why and what to try first.
- **One question a week**: an agent may ask you something it can't tell from your data. Tap an answer, add a note if you want, or skip. It remembers your answer for 90 days. It never changes anything on its own.
- **Spending cap**: a monthly limit ($20 to start) across both your devices, with a warning at 80%. Automatic runs pause at the cap, and anything you start asks first.
- **Safer suggestions**: the app now rejects changes that clash (for example, adding a deload during a deload, or adding sets next to an injury flag).
- **Smarter progression**: weights no longer jump after one outlier session, and a jump earned on grinders repeats once first. These only ever hold a weight back, never push it up.

---

## Safety and fixes

- Typed reps survive repaints, the effort slider drags properly, and a workout that runs past midnight survives a reload.
- Backups no longer carry your API key or tokens.
- Cut-off AI replies are now counted in your spend.
- Fixed a bug where logging a weigh-in could hide the sleep section until the next refresh.
- The info (ⓘ), 1RM and edit buttons on the current lift in Live can be tapped again. They were tiny, and the big weight number was swallowing the tap. They're now proper thumb-sized buttons.
- On Fuel, the calorie and protein range you tap now lights up in its colour with readable numbers. It was showing dark text on a dark button.
- Lying Leg Curl: every past set had been logged per side, so all of them (and the PR) are now doubled to the real total. It goes up 10 lb at a time, and Live shows the plates for each side. Log the total from now on.
